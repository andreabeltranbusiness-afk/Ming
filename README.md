# 🌾 Ming: Know the Price. Negotiate Better.

Ming means clarity. It is a simple tool that helps you learn more about market prices before selling your products, so you can negotiate with more information and work toward a fairer price.

---

## 🚜 Have you ever had this problem?
* It is time to sell your harvest, but you don't know how much your product is selling for in other places.
* Sometimes, the buyer or middleman knows more about market prices than you do. Because of this, you may end up selling your product for less than it is worth.
* If you live in a rural area, it can also be difficult to find price information because you may have limited internet access or only a basic phone.

**Ming is here to help.**

---

## 💡 How can Ming help you?
With Ming, you can check market price information in a simple way. You don't need internet all the time.

### 📱 If you have a basic phone
You can use SMS or USSD to check information. You don't need a smartphone. For example:
* Dial **`*123#`**
* You can also send a text message to make a query.

### 📲 If you have a smartphone
* You can check the information directly from your phone.
* The latest price information can be saved on your phone, so you can check it even when you don't have internet.
* When you have a connection again, the information can be updated.

---

## 💰 How can this help you sell?
* Before selling your product, you can use Ming to get a reference price.
* This gives you more information when talking with a buyer and can help you negotiate with greater confidence.
* **Ming does not decide how much you should sell your product for. The decision is always yours.**

---

## ⚠️ What if there is not enough information?
Ming will not make up a price. 

If the information is too old or there is not enough information about your product or area, Ming will tell you:
> *"There is not enough information to evaluate the price in this area."*

This helps you know when the information may not be enough to make a decision.

---

## 🔒 Your information is protected
Ming is designed to use as little personal information as possible. You do not need to provide:
* Your name
* Your ID number
* Your address
* A personal profile

Your query can be made anonymously. Your phone number is not saved unless you ask for help from a person and agree to receive a call.

---

## 🌱 Built with your reality in mind
We know that not every farmer has a smartphone or internet access all the time. That is why Ming is designed to work even in places where the internet connection is limited.

You deserve to have information before you sell your products.

🌾 **More information.**  
🤝 **Better negotiation.**  
💰 **A more informed decision.**
